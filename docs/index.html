A score overlay that would rather stay blank than be wrong.

Qoresence reads your capture card and shows the score on your stream overlay only when it can confirm it; otherwise the overlay stays blank instead of showing a wrong score.

Who it's for

PS5 streamers playing Madden NFL 27 or College Football 27 with a capture card and a Windows laptop.

What you need
  • An HDMI capture card between the PS5 and the laptop
  • A Windows 10 or 11 laptop with Python 3.11 or newer
  • OBS, to show the overlay as a Browser Source (Qoresence keeps the capture card; OBS never opens it)
  • Optional: a DualSense the laptop can see (USB), so your button presses line up with the video
Local observatory

Goes dark instead of lying.

One capture card. The video and your DualSense presses share one clock. The score stays blank until a confirm check (a ConfirmTicket) matches it to the picture. Button icons follow the picture — they do not invent it.

Same HDMI

Different job.

Most tools overlay a stream. Qoresence keeps one session memory and hangs glasses off it.

Card

Opens once

One owner for the physical card: Qoresence. Every view (a “glass”) reads the same shared frames (FrameHub). OBS and everything else never open the device; OBS only gets the Browser Source overlay.

Clock

One monotonic spine

HDMI frames and DualSense edges share clock_ns. Video clock, pad clock, and chat clock do not live in three programs.

Speech

Tickets, not guesses

Score digits need the score reader to lock on (score_vlm_locked) and a ConfirmTicket — a confirm check against the same frame. A lock alone stays blank. A menu, a used-up quota, or a missing API key becomes one plain sentence instead of a number.

Clips

Local MP4 + sidecars

Capture-ring files stay on the box. Coupling, buttons, and OTel traces ride the same clock. No clip API as the product.

Stick

Idle is honest

BT DualSense Edge is the play pipe. USB into the laptop is observe-only. Glyphs couple to the HDMI clock. A still pad is presence evidence, not a fail.

The capture card is the brain. Everything else is a glass.
